Privacy

Cairn stores the issues, comments and account details your team creates so the product can show them back to you. We do not sell that data or use it to train models.

We use one first-party analytics counter for page views on this site and no advertising trackers.

You can export everything as CSV or JSON from workspace settings, and deleting a workspace removes its data from our primary database within 7 days and from backups within 35 days.

Questions go to privacy@cairn.dev.
